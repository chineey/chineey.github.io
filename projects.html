X Job Application Agent

Finds job posts on X and applies for me.

An autonomous agent that watches X for software job posts, reads the details (including ones posted as images, using Gemini vision), and tailors a fresh PDF resume for each role. It then sends the application from my Gmail, or attaches my own resume unchanged if I prefer.

Posts that use an application form or link are logged and sent to me on Telegram, along with failures and a pause command. If a recruiter replies or a week passes in silence, it drafts the response for me to send. It runs serverless on Vercel cron, and I'm building it with a second developer on GitHub.

SLEEK Magazine

Independent digital publication and culture magazine, live in production.

A production editorial and digital publishing platform covering Nigerian fashion, art direction, music and emerging creators. It serves around 200 daily visitors, and I build, ship and maintain the whole thing myself, including the database. I also manage its SEO, from Search Console setup and indexing to metadata and structured content.

Readers get a cover hero and editorial feed, subscriber-only articles behind a Paystack-powered paywall, event ratings and comments, and a shop for physical and digital merchandise with pickup locations and emailed purchase details. Editors run it through a custom admin CMS where they publish articles, swap covers and images, and control where each story sits on the page.

Built on the Next.js 14 App Router with server rendering and API routes, React 18, and PostgreSQL through Prisma for schema design and migrations. The look is a chrome and copper industrial theme.
